🧠 Interview Logic Drill
Q1: What number replaces the question mark in the series: 2, 9, 28, 65, ?
Answer: 126. Pattern: n³ + 1. (1³+1=2, 2³+1=9, 3³+1=28, 4³+1=65, 5³+1=126)
